What Zap Books does for you

  • Expenses in order, receipts attached

    Enter an expense, attach the photo or PDF, or import payments from your bank's CSV. Possible duplicates and missing details are pointed out before anything is posted.

  • Clear numbers for you and your accountant

    Spending by month and category, balances for bank, card, loan and owner accounts, and CSV exports. Invite your accountant as a read-only viewer.

  • Every change on the record

    Each approved change is signed and linked to the one before. Corrections keep the original and its reversal visible, so nothing quietly disappears.

Also built in: owner loans, contributions and expenses you paid personally. If your business holds bitcoin, keep those books in sats, with the dollar value recorded at each entry. No bitcoin needed to use Zap Books.

Available now

Run AI locally

A receipt reader that runs on your own computer. It reads receipts; it isn't a chat assistant.

What it does
Reads a receipt photo or PDF and suggests the vendor, date, total, tax, tip, fees, discounts, receipt number and a category from your list. Each suggestion shows the printed text it came from.
Where it runs
On your computer. The browser hands the receipt to the reader on your machine, which asks a vision model that also runs there. The reader refuses a model on any other computer.
What it sees
The receipt you send it and your category names. Never your books.
Setup
Technical: Ollama, a 20 GB model and about 22 GB of free memory (a smaller model needs about 6 GB). Setup guide.
Turn it off
Stop the reader. Read receipt switches off and everything else keeps working.
Coming next · not available yet

Bring your own AI

Connect an AI agent you already use, and choose your own model for reading receipts.

What it will do
Your agent joins the business like a person, with its own key and the AI assistant role. It can create drafts and attach suggestions. It can't approve, correct, import or change settings.
Where it runs
Wherever you run it: on your computer, or with an outside AI provider you pick.
What it sees
Your books, within the history you choose when you add it.
Ready today
The assistant role and its limits, enforced by the service. The tools that connect an agent aren't available yet, and no providers are supported yet.
Turn it off
Remove it like any member. It's refused at once, and new records use a new key.

An API key isn't the same as local. If your assistant uses an outside provider, that provider receives what the assistant reads or sends, which can include your books. Their terms decide how it's kept and whether it's used for training. Zap Books can't promise anything on their behalf.

Practical jobs, not vague promises

What AI does in Zap Books today, next to what the assistant is being built to do.

Helpful AI. Human approval. Verifiable records.

From receipt to approved entry, each step has its own protection, and each protection is enforced by the software, not just shown on screen.

  1. 1 · Add

    Bring in the expense

    Type it in, attach a receipt photo or PDF, or import a bank or card CSV. Imports arrive as drafts.

  2. 2 · Prepare

    AI helps prepare

    The receipt reader, or your saved rules, suggest details with their evidence. You accept, edit or ignore each one.

    Enforced: the reader can't reach your books and has no key to sign with. The AI assistant role can only draft and suggest; the service refuses its approvals.

  3. 3 · Approve

    Authorized people approve

    Owners and bookkeepers approve. Viewers only read. Approval records the expense; it never sends money.

    Enforced: every change is a request signed with the member's own key, and the service checks the signer's role before accepting it.

  4. 4 · Record

    Signed history keeps the trail

    The accepted change is signed and linked to the one before. Totals, balances and exports update.

    Enforced: the app checks the whole chain when it loads your books. A changed, removed or reordered entry makes the check fail.

Know who can access your books. Know what changed.

What Zap Books does to protect your records, and where the limits are.

Read the security model See the source code

  • Sign in with a key you control

    No password. Your key stays in your signer app, and Zap Books never asks for the secret part of it.

  • Role-based permissions

    Owners, bookkeepers who record and approve, submitters who send receipts, viewers who only read, and an AI assistant role that can never approve.

  • Encrypted storage

    Receipt files are encrypted in your browser before upload. Each change is encrypted for your members. The service keeps a working copy it can read, on an encrypted disk.

  • Signed change history

    Every accepted change is signed and linked to the one before, and checked each time your books load.

  • Recovery and sensitive-change protection

    Every business has a second owner or an offline backup key. Changes to owners or keys wait 48 hours and can be vetoed.

Who can read what

Members you add
According to their role and the history you give them. Someone you remove can't read new records, but keeps anything they already saw.
The Zap Books service
Reads each change to check it against the accounting rules before signing it. The service keeps a working copy it can read, on an encrypted disk, so encryption doesn't hide your books from the service.
The local receipt reader
Only the receipt you send it, on your own computer. The details you accept become part of the expense, like anything you type.
An AI assistant (coming next)
Your books, within the history you choose, wherever that assistant runs.
What “verifiable” does and doesn't mean

Verification checks the history: that every accepted change was signed by your business's service, links to the change before it, and came from a signed request by a member. If a stored entry is changed, removed or reordered, the check fails.

It doesn't check that the bookkeeping itself is right. A wrong category or amount that someone approved is recorded faithfully, and is fixed with a correction that keeps both versions visible.

Keys, recovery and access

Every business needs a way back in: a second owner or an offline backup key, chosen when you create it.

Changes to owners, the backup key or the service's key wait 48 hours, and another owner or the backup key can veto them. Because a backup key can take over after 48 hours, keep it as safe as your own key.

Removing someone changes the encryption keys for new records. Records they already saw or downloaded can't be recalled.

Questions

Who is Zap Books for?

Independent businesses, freelancers and small teams who want clear expense records they control, especially owners who lend or put their own money into the business. If you need invoicing, payroll or sales tax, you'll still need other tools for now.

What can I do during the beta?

Everything listed as available under What's ready: expenses, receipts, owner money, CSV imports, reports, exports and inviting members. Beta members also help decide what comes next. The beta limitations listed there apply.

Do I have to use AI?

No. Every bookkeeping task works without it. The receipt reader is optional and runs on your own computer once you set it up. If it isn't running, you enter the details yourself, and saved vendor rules can still suggest a category and purpose.

Does Zap Books send my books to an AI provider?

No. Zap Books has no cloud AI. The receipt reader runs on your computer and sees only the receipt you send it. When bringing your own AI arrives, you'll decide whether to connect an assistant and where it runs. An assistant that uses an outside provider sends that provider what it reads, so check that provider's terms.

Can my accountant use it?

Yes. Add them as a viewer so they can read your records without changing them, or send them CSV exports of your expenses, the full journal and account balances. They'll need their own Nostr key to sign in.

Can I import and export my records?

You can import bank and card payments, and paid-expense history exported from tools like QuickBooks or Zoho Books, as CSV files. Imports create drafts for you to review. You can export expenses, the journal and balances as CSV at any time. Full migration of complete books, and a complete backup-and-restore file, aren't available yet.

Do I need bitcoin or to know about Nostr?

You don't need bitcoin. You do need a Nostr key and a signer extension in a desktop browser to sign in. If you've never used one, we'll help you set it up. You can keep books in sats today, with the dollar value recorded at the time of each entry. Cost basis and gains aren't tracked yet; they're coming next.

Who can read my books?

The members you add, according to their role, and the Zap Books service, which reads each change to check it before recording it. Receipt files are encrypted in your browser before upload. Each change is encrypted for your members. The service keeps a working copy it can read, on an encrypted disk. Someone you remove can't read new records, but keeps anything they already saw.

What happens if I lose my key?

Every business is set up with a second owner or an offline backup key. A backup key can replace your key after a 48-hour wait. A second owner can remove your old key and add a new one, also after 48 hours. If you lose your key and that backup, nobody can restore your access, including us. Keep the backup key offline and safe.

What happens if Zap Books disappears?

Export your records to CSV whenever you like, and keep those copies yourself. Zap Books is open source under the MIT license, so you can also run it yourself. Keeping a verified copy of your books on a relay you run is planned, not available yet.

Is this tax or accounting advice?

No. Zap Books helps you keep accurate records. For tax and accounting decisions, talk to a qualified professional.
